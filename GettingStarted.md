These first steps will set you up for success in CYBER 210

 * Connect to the BYU Cybersecurity Discord Server CYBER 210 Channel
	* Browse to [https://discord.gg/QNtYkyHUKR"](https://discord.gg/QNtYkyHUKR){:target="_blank"}
		* Assign yourself roles for classes: Go to the **Channels & Roles** tool above your channels list. Then scroll down to "What IT&C classes are you in this semester?" Pick "210" (or 210A / 210B).
		* Update your Discord server nickname to read `Firstname Lastname \| Nickname` (e.g., `Thag Simmons \| thagomizer`). Or just `Firstname and Lastname`. To do this, click on "BYU Cybersecurity" in the top left, "Edit Per-server Profile", then change your "Server Nickname".
		* Introduce yourself on the ITC210 Slack Channel. We suggest:
            * Your name
			* Your major
			* Something you hope to learn in the class
			* Something you like.
* Review the [Computer Requirements]() and form a strategy for how you will address them. You always have the option of using the lab computers.
* Review all of the CYBER 210 information on LearningSuite. Pay special attention to the schedule tab.
* Create an account on [Github.com](http://GitHub.com){:target="_blank"} if you do not have one already.
* Review the syllabus and take the Syllabus and Policy Quiz.
* Look at your first homework assignment, due Friday.
    * CLI is short for Command-Line Interface.
    * The TAs will introduce this assignment at the beginning of the lab period on the first day of class.

## Installing Key Tools:

* Follow these instructions to [Install Visual Studio Code and Git](/InstallCodeAndGit){:target="_blank"}
* If you use Windows, follow these instructions to [Install WSL2 and Docker](/InstallWsl2AndDocker){:target="_blank"}
* If you use a Mac, follow these instructions to [Install Docker](https://docs.docker.com/docker-for-mac/install/){:target="_blank"}

*In this class we use contemporary web technologies often hosted in the cloud. The providers of the tools we use make regular updates to address security vulnerabilities, improve performance, or add features. We (your professor and TAs) strive to stay on top of changes and update the course materials accordingly, but sometimes we are surprised by a mid-semester change. Please watch the Slack channel for announcements of updates to instructions or workarounds for unforeseen issues.*

*We do not apologize for this. It's how the real world of computing technology works. Things are fast-moving and frequently changing. Among the goals of this class is for you to become familiar with that dynamic.*
